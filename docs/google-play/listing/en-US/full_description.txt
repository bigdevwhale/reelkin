Something sleeps in a film can. Log three films and the can opens: the genres decide who climbs out. Noir gives a cat, horror a bat, animation a fox. Every minute you watch helps the pet grow, and every film is a ticket stub and a spectator in a hundred-seat hall. Free, no ads, no account, your diary stays on your phone.

🎞️ THE BOOTH
• Put a film on the projector: the lights go down, the reels turn and the pet watches from the port, jumping at horror and laughing at comedy
• When the credits roll you tear a stub: popcorn from one to five, three words, who you watched with, a post-credits scene, and the day you watched it
• The third film of a genre gives the pet a trait: a fedora for noir, an antenna for sci-fi, a red nose for comedy, white gloves for animation

🍿 THE HALL
• A hundred velvet seats fill with spectators dressed by genre: detectives, cowboys, ghosts, astronauts. Tap one to see the stub
• The sign: each film gives the first letter of its title, and the cinema's name is built only from the letters you own
• Decades fill the booth with new gear, countries leave stamps, and the year's awards you hand out yourself

📺 SERIES
• A season ticket from any season: an episode tears a ticket, «cliffhanger!» marks the worst moment, a finale seats a spectator
• See how many seasons are out and when the next one starts
• A dropped series is simply taken off, no guilt

🗂️ THE BILL
• What's waiting, what's running and every stub, with a filter by genre, popcorn, year and company
• Stats: films by month and year, hours, genres, decades, countries, weekdays
• Search by title brings the poster, year, country and length from Wikipedia

🎲 WHAT'S ON?
• Say how much time you have and what you're in the mood for, and the pet picks from the bill what fits, favouring a decade or a country you don't have yet. There's a double bill too

🧳 BRING YOUR HISTORY
• Series from a public MyShows profile: finished seasons become stubs, the one you're watching goes on at the episode you reached
• Ratings from Kinopoisk, read off its own pages, with the rating and the date

ALSO
• The buffet sells string lights, a velvet armchair, a «HUSH» neon and a popcorn machine for popcorn
• Real posters or pixel ones, and a dimmed photo behind the screens if you like
• English and Russian
• Free and open source (MIT)

Privacy: no account, no servers of ours, no analytics, no ads. Your films, stubs and pet stay on your phone. The app goes online only when you search for a film, open a series (to check its seasons) or bring your history over.
