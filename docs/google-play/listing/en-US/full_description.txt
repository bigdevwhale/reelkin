Something sleeps in every film can. Three cans are open from the start: a detective cat, an animator fox and a critic owl. The other 46 open by the way you watch: horror, westerns, films after midnight, old films, rewatches, series binged. Not only animals: a little dragon, a unicorn, a phoenix, a kitsune, a back-row ghost, and 17 anime pets, from a tanuki to a mecha. Every minute you watch helps the pet grow, and every film is a ticket stub and a spectator in a hundred-seat hall. Free, no ads, no account, your diary stays on your phone.

🎞️ THE BOOTH
• Put a film on the projector: the lights go down, the reels turn and the pet watches from the port, jumping at horror and laughing at comedy
• When the credits roll you tear a stub: popcorn from one to five, three words, who you watched with, a post-credits scene, and the day you watched it
• Tonight: pick a mood, and the pet offers a film from the bill that fits the evening, with a few more to swap to

🐾 49 PETS
• From a film can to a little one, half-grown, grown-up and old-timer
• Each has favourite films that feed it one and a half times as much and pay popcorn
• A nature of its own: a greedy one grows faster, a curious one is paid for new countries. Grown up, it takes a form for good from what it was fed
• Only the one in the booth eats; call the others back any time. One in sixteen comes out in technicolor

📖 THE SAGA
• A week, a month, a year or all time: hours, best films and series, genres, countries, decades, the hour you watch most, records and a calendar of the year
• Goals picked from a couple of dozen, weekly tasks, and 88 achievements in steps, each step paying popcorn

💌 POSTCARDS
• A stub's review, an achievement, the pet, the booth, the year in squares, genres, the menagerie: as a post or a story, with a QR code if you like

🍿 THE HALL
• A hundred velvet seats fill with spectators dressed by genre: detectives, cowboys, ghosts, astronauts. Tap one to see the stub
• Decades fill the booth with new gear, countries leave stamps, and the year's awards you hand out yourself

📺 SERIES
• A season ticket from any season: an episode tears a ticket, «cliffhanger!» marks the worst moment, a finale seats a spectator
• See how many seasons are out and when the next one starts
• A dropped series is simply taken off, no guilt

🗂️ THE BILL
• What's waiting, what's running and a wall of stubs by month, with search and filters by genre, popcorn, year, company, directors and cast
• Search by title brings the poster, year, country, length, director and cast from Wikipedia and Wikidata

🧳 BRING YOUR HISTORY
• Series from a public MyShows profile: finished seasons become stubs, the one you're watching goes on at the episode you reached
• Ratings from Kinopoisk, read off its own pages, with the rating and the date

ALSO
• The buffet sells wallpapers for the booth, string lights, a velvet armchair, a «HUSH» neon, a popcorn machine, a rug, a plant and a clock, and lets you try each on first
• English and Russian
• Free and open source (MIT)

Privacy: no account, no servers of ours, no analytics, no ads. Your films, stubs and pets stay on your phone. The app goes online only when you search for a film, open a series (to check its seasons) or bring your history over.
